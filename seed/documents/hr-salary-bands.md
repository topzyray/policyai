# FY26 Salary Bands

This restricted Human Resources record sets Northline pay bands for the coming fiscal year. It is visible to the HR manager and to people explicitly granted viewer access. A general HR employee must not receive this record through PolicyAI.

Managers may approve an offer inside the published band. Offers above the band require the HR manager and the function leader. Do not quote these ranges in a department-wide channel.

The compensation committee reviews the bands each March. Equity refreshes are separate from base pay and are not listed here.

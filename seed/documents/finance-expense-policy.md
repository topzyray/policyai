# Expense Policy

This internal policy tells Finance department members how to file expenses. It does not contain revenue figures or salary information.

EXPENSE_POLICY_INTERNAL_3308

Employees submit expenses within 30 days. Meals on an overnight trip are capped at 75 per day. Software purchases above 500 need a Finance partner's approval before the vendor is paid. Receipts are attached in the expense tool, not sent through chat.

Managers approve their team's reports. Finance reviews anything over 2,000 and any international travel.

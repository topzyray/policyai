# Employee Handbook

This internal handbook is for members of Human Resources and the employees they support. It describes leave, conduct in the workplace, and how to ask for a policy exception.

HANDBOOK_PTO_INTERNAL_4410

Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid time off each year, plus local public holidays. Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver. Requests go to the employee's manager and are confirmed by HR.

Remote employees keep a home workspace that meets the safety checklist. Northline provides a monitor and a chair. Travel to a Northline office for a team week is booked through the travel desk.

Questions about this handbook can be sent to the HR operations queue. Salary bands, bonus targets, and individual compensation are not part of this handbook.
